# Impeccable design context — Warung Pintar

## Design Context
(Session 2 — rebuilt from the owner's actual answers + visual reference, replacing the
session-1 self-answered version.)

### Users
Small F&B/retail owners in Southeast Asia (primary persona: an Indonesian café owner,
low digital literacy, runs the business from WhatsApp on a mid-range Android; checks the
dashboard on a laptop/tablet in the evening). Staff use a shared kiosk at a bright
counter.

### Brand Personality (user-confirmed)
**Warung hangat** — warm, personal, slightly informal; "kelola usahamu, makin mudah".
The microcopy already has this voice; the visuals must match it. Confidence comes from
big honest numbers, warmth from color and shape — never from clutter.

### Aesthetic Direction (user-confirmed via reference image, 2026-07-07)
Warm-light iOS glass:
- **Background**: warm off-white with a soft ambient wash; frosted white cards with
  gentle shadows and generous (24px) radii. Light is the primary experience; the dark
  variant follows the same warmth (deep green-tinted, no purple). POS stays forced-light.
- **Accent**: forest/sage **green** (supersedes the brief's original blue-violet — owner
  override via reference). Amber for warnings, soft red for negatives. Money-positive is
  the green itself.
- **Typography**: Inter (locked), with a real scale — display-size rupiah numbers,
  tabular figures; small-caps eyebrows used sparingly.
- **Composition** (user-confirmed): moderate desktop density with true multi-column
  layouts; **one hero glass surface per page** — everything else is open rows on the
  page background or quiet plates. Lists are hairline-separated rows inside a group,
  never per-row pill cards.
- **Chronology is structure**: transaction/expense/alert lists are grouped by day with
  strong day headers ("Hari ini", "Kemarin", "6 Jul") + per-day totals; rows are quiet.
- Stat cards follow the reference grammar: icon tile → label + HelpTip → big number →
  delta chip → mini sparkline.
- Item rows get a small warm tile (initials/unit mark; no product photos in the data),
  quantity pill colored by risk, secondary "Minimum X" line, chevron.

### Design Principles
1. **One glance, one answer** — each page leads with the single number the owner came for.
2. **One hero surface per page** — glass is earned, not wallpaper.
3. **The day is the unit of history** — day headers do the scanning work, rows stay quiet.
4. **Numbers are the typography** — display-scale rupiah, 1.25+ type ratio.
5. **Warm, never loud** — green accent at 10% visual weight; warmth via surface tint,
   radius, and microcopy, not decoration.
