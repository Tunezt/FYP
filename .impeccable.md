# Impeccable design context — Warung Pintar

## Design Context

### Users
Small F&B/retail owners in Southeast Asia (primary persona: an Indonesian café owner in her 40s–50s, low digital literacy, runs the business from WhatsApp on a mid-range Android). Staff are young, part-time, using a shared kiosk at a bright shop counter. The dashboard is the owner's occasional "sit down and look at the business" surface — evenings, often on a phone.

### Brand Personality
Capable, warm, unfussy. Like a sharp assistant who texts you back fast. Three words: **tenang, rapi, jelas** (calm, tidy, clear). Emotional goal: "wait, this looks really good" followed by "…and I understand everything on it."

### Aesthetic Direction (LOCKED by project brief — overrides skill defaults where they conflict)
iOS-Native Glass: light/dark-adaptive frosted panels (`backdrop-blur`), Inter/SF Pro typography, restrained blue-violet gradient accent, sheet-style modals, segmented controls, card-based layout. The POS route is forced to a light high-contrast variant.
- Note: the impeccable skill bans Inter and glassmorphism as reflex defaults; here they are an explicit, locked project decision made after mockup comparison — keep them, but apply the skill's *craft* discipline: glass used purposefully (not on every element), varied card treatments, no identical stat-card grids, no side-stripe borders, no gradient text, real spacing rhythm, considered empty states.
- Microcopy: Bahasa Indonesia first (the audience's language), short and human.

### Design Principles
1. **One glance, one answer.** Every screen leads with the single number/fact the owner came for; detail is secondary.
2. **Glass is a material, not a wallpaper.** Frosted panels for elevated surfaces only; quiet tinted flats elsewhere.
3. **Numbers are the typography.** Tabular figures, big and confident; labels small and quiet.
4. **Nothing needs a manual.** Indonesian microcopy, help affordances on jargon (anomali, velocity), empty states that teach.
5. **Fast over fancy.** Parallel fetches, skeletons, optimistic acks; no animation longer than 400ms.
