# Informed consent: text for the trial participant

For CP1 §3.8. The participant (the owner, Ibu Diah, and any staff who take part) reads it and
confirms either on paper or by replying **SETUJU** on WhatsApp. Keep the paper copy or a
screenshot of the reply with the date. Nobody is pressed: the answer **TIDAK** ends it and
changes nothing about how they use the system.

## Indonesian (send or print this)

> **Persetujuan ikut uji coba Poernama**
>
> Halo, saya [nama peneliti]. Saya sedang menyelesaikan proyek akhir kuliah: sebuah asisten bisnis
> untuk kafe lewat WhatsApp, layar kasir, dan dasbor web. Saya mau mengajak Anda mencoba
> sistem ini selama 2 sampai 4 minggu, lalu mengisi kuesioner singkat (10 pertanyaan,
> sekitar 5 menit) dan ngobrol sebentar tentang pengalaman Anda.
>
> Yang akan saya catat: pesan yang Anda kirim ke asisten, apakah jawabannya berhasil atau
> harus diulang, dan data penjualan yang memang sudah dicatat sistem. Data ini hanya untuk
> menilai sistem dan ditulis di laporan tanpa nama Anda dan tanpa angka keuangan kafe yang
> bisa dikenali, kecuali Anda mengizinkannya.
>
> Ikut atau tidak, sepenuhnya pilihan Anda. Anda boleh berhenti kapan saja tanpa alasan,
> dan data uji coba Anda akan saya hapus kalau diminta. Tidak ada biaya, dan Anda tidak
> mendapat bayaran. Sistem ini masih diuji, jadi jawabannya bisa salah: angka penting tetap
> dicek di dasbor.
>
> Kalau Anda setuju, balas **SETUJU**. Kalau tidak, balas **TIDAK**.

## English (for the report's appendix)

> **Consent to take part in the Poernama trial**
>
> I am completing a final-year project: a business assistant for cafes over WhatsApp, a till
> screen and a web dashboard. I invite you to use it for 2 to 4 weeks, then fill in a short
> questionnaire (10 questions, about 5 minutes) and talk briefly about your experience.
>
> I will record: the messages you send to the assistant, whether the answer worked or had to
> be repeated, and the sales data the system already stores. The data is used only to
> evaluate the system and appears in the report without your name and without
> recognisable financial figures, unless you allow it.
>
> Taking part is your choice. You may stop at any time without giving a reason, and I will
> delete your trial data if you ask. There is no cost and no payment. The system is still
> being tested and can be wrong: check important figures on the dashboard.
>
> If you agree, reply **SETUJU**. If not, reply **TIDAK**.
